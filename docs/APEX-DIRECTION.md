# Apex Direction Contract

THESIS: A kinetic, full-screen technical portfolio where the work is experienced as a sequence of systems, not browsed as a grid. It refuses the centered résumé template.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black stage, high-contrast electric cyan as the single signal color, white condensed display type, mono telemetry labels, thin orbit/trajectory lines, hard-edged media frames, and restrained grain. No gradients-as-decoration, no glass cards, no generic dashboard chrome.

STORY: The visitor feels a point of view immediately, understands that the work concerns inspectable AI systems, then moves through project artifacts as a controlled sequence toward evidence, writing, and contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Fixed utility navigation at top; oversized left-anchored statement across the center; a living orbit/trace field behind it; project counter and scroll cue anchored low; primary action is the work index, visible without competing with the thesis.
