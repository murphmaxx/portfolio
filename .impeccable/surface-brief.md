# Apex Homepage Surface Brief

## Scope and mode

Experience-mode homepage prototype for the Apex visual direction. This surface is an immersive portfolio entry point, not the final project case-file implementation.

## Audience and job

Recruiters and hiring managers should quickly recognize a serious AI security/safety engineering direction, understand that the portfolio is evidence-led, and know where to enter the work.

## Chosen direction

Apex: near-black stage, electric cyan signal, condensed display type, mono telemetry labels, orbit/trace field, oversized left-anchored statement, and a sticky project run.

## Visitor path

Drive → Signal → Project run → Writing → Contact.

## Signature interaction

Scroll changes the page from an opening drive into a pinned sequence of project frames. The field and frame movement provide orientation without making content depend on motion.

## Constraints

No invented claims or metrics. Placeholder content must remain visibly provisional until source material is reviewed. Core navigation and project access must remain usable with keyboard, touch, reduced motion, and motion disabled. Static deployment remains possible.

## Unresolved

Final public copy, project media, exact case-file routes, production framework, and demo-video capture workflow.
