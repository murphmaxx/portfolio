# Field background asset prompt

## Prompt

Create a wide 16:9 high-resolution abstract technical environment for a premium defense-industrial AI systems portfolio. The scene is a dark British racing green operations room / computational field viewed from above at shallow perspective: layered matte composite panels, a central circular sensor aperture, subtle bronze alloy machined rings, low-contrast Nardo grey technical markings, fine measurement ticks, sparse cable-like trajectories, and deep controlled shadows. No people, no weapons, no flags, no logos, no words, no UI panels, no glowing neon, no cyberpunk, no sci-fi holograms, no bright green. Cinematic but restrained. Photoreal industrial materials, physically plausible lighting, expensive aerospace/automotive engineering finish, large quiet negative space in the left-center for white typography, detail concentrated toward the right and lower field. The image must read as a real physical technical environment, not a poster or illustration.

## Intended use

Full-bleed fixed background for the portfolio hero and project scroll field. Text overlays remain semantic HTML. The background is atmosphere and depth, not evidence of a real facility or product.

## Required output

- landscape 16:9
- minimum 2400×1350 pixels
- dark exposure with readable overlay zones
- no embedded lettering or logos
- preserve the broad negative-space region on left-center
